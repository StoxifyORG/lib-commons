Add Analyst Acquisition Funnel types:
- `AnalystAcquisitionStage`: 5-stage funnel states (PENDING_VERIFICATION, APPROVED, ACTIVATED, FIRST_SUBSCRIBER, SILENT).
- `AnalystAcquisitionRow`: Lead pipeline row with sales contact details (phone, email, company, location, website, linkedin, twitter, experience, specialization, registration type) and activation metrics.
- `AnalystAcquisitionSummary`: Aggregated funnel milestones, conversion rates, and median activation days.
